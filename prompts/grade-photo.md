# Grade one prepared GekiGrade job

Inspect `source.json`, `analysis.json`, `preview.jpg`, `crops/contact-sheet.jpg`, `crops/candidates.json`, `looks.json`, and `edit-plan.schema.json` inside the job. Compare all prepared crop anchors and select by visible composition; anchor names are geometric positions, not subject-detection claims.

Create exactly three restrained candidates: conservative correction, recommended look, and alternative look. Use an explicit supported edit-plan version and only its permitted fields. `edit-plan-1.0.0.schema.json` preserves the legacy ACEScct shoulder. `edit-plan-2.0.0.schema.json` replaces the global `highlight_rolloff` with required `shadow_recovery_ev` (0–2 stops, fades out at 18% linear luminance) and `highlight_compression` (0–1, rational luminance shoulder above 50%). Use `plans/example-plan-v2.json` as a structural example for version 2. Do not mechanically reinterpret legacy shoulder values; the parameters are different. Version 2 uses fixed output-gamut compression with no planner control. Keep shadow recovery at zero when silhouettes or deep shadows are intentional. Neither operation reconstructs missing detail.

Reference existing crop and look IDs/versions exactly. Look definitions keep their existing math. Treat `temperature_mired_shift` as post-development chromatic adaptation for JPEG and prepared RAW, not a RAW white-balance control. Do not emit prose, commands, paths, masks, or unknown operations in the plan. Save only schema-valid JSON to the job's `plans` directory, then run `geki validate-plan` before rendering.

After rendering inspect stage QA as well as the photographs. Compare global versus creative stages on the full frame, and pre-gamut versus pre-clamp stages on the output crop. Do not compare percentages across different scopes as if they represented the same pixels. Check extrema to distinguish tiny floating-point excursions from visible clipping; check actual decoded-JPEG clipping after sharpening and compression. Do not brighten a silhouette or weaken a look merely to force metrics to zero.
